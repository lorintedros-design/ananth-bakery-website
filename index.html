Our story
Three generations · One legacy

A family tradition,
baked to be shared.

What began as a small dream in 1968 has grown into a familiar part of life in Sawyerpuram. Our founder, Mr. V. Thiruvadi, built Ananth Bakery on a simple promise: honest quality, hard work and the comfort of something made with care.

That promise was carried forward by Mr. Mohan and Mr. Altrin. Today, the next generation continues the journey—thankful for every customer who has made our bakery part of their family’s memories.
